Every open ride in Manhattan, dispatched perfectly

Manhattan. Friday, June 5, 2015, 6:03 PM. 1,024 open ride requests (white), 1,152 free cabs (yellow). Real positions from the public NYC TLC record, the last era with exact GPS coordinates in the data. Free-cab positions are the previous 12 minutes' drop-offs. Assigning every cab to a rider at once, for the fewest total miles, is a linear program with over a million variables: one per possible cab-and-rider pairing.

This browser has no WebGPU, so it can't run the solve. You'll need Chrome/Edge 113+, Firefox 141+, or Safari 26+. Here's what it looks like:

Solved live by Sundial, an LP solver running as WebGPU compute shaders in your browser. "Optimal" is only ever claimed after an independent CPU-f64 re-verification of the exact answer returned. Two caveats worth naming: distances are straight-line, and "free cab" is a proxy built from recent drop-offs. Nothing leaves your machine.
